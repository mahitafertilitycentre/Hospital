About Mahita Fertility & IVF Centre

Personalised fertility and women's healthcare in Hyderabad

Mahita Fertility & IVF Centre is dedicated to providing comprehensive fertility, reproductive medicine, obstetric and gynaecological care. We understand that every woman's health journey and every couple's parenthood journey is unique.

Our approach focuses on proper evaluation, individual treatment planning, patient education and compassionate support at every stage. From fertility assessment and assisted reproductive techniques to pregnancy care and women's health, our goal is to help patients understand their options and make informed decisions.

We believe in combining professional medical guidance with personal attention, dignity and respect.

Fertility & Advanced IVF Treatments

Comprehensive evaluation and treatment options for infertility

🌸

Infertility Evaluation

Assessment of female and male fertility, medical history, ovulation and reproductive health to guide treatment planning.

🧬

IVF Treatment

In Vitro Fertilisation involving egg retrieval, laboratory fertilisation, embryo development and planned embryo transfer.

💗

IUI Treatment

Intrauterine Insemination using prepared sperm placed into the uterus around ovulation when suitable.

🔬

ICSI Treatment

Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection, a specialised laboratory technique in which a single sperm is injected into an egg.

🧪

IMSI

Intracytoplasmic Morphologically Selected Sperm Injection, a specialised sperm selection approach considered in selected cases.

🩺

Ovulation Assessment

Evaluation of menstrual cycles, ovulation and follicular development to support fertility planning.

🔍

PESA

Percutaneous Epididymal Sperm Aspiration, a sperm retrieval technique for selected male infertility conditions.

🧬

TESA

Testicular Sperm Aspiration, a sperm retrieval procedure considered after appropriate male fertility assessment.

🌱

Male Infertility

Evaluation and guidance for sperm-related fertility concerns and appropriate treatment options.

❄️

Fertility Preservation

Consultation about egg, sperm and embryo preservation options where medically appropriate.

🩻

Hysteroscopy

Evaluation of the uterine cavity and selected procedures for conditions affecting reproductive health.

🏥

Laparoscopy

Minimally invasive assessment and surgical management of selected gynaecological and pelvic conditions.

Pregnancy Planning

Gynaecology & Women's Healthcare

Comprehensive care at every stage of a woman's life

🌺

PCOS / PCOD Treatment

Evaluation and management of polycystic ovary syndrome, hormonal concerns and menstrual irregularities.

🩸

Menstrual Disorders

Consultation for irregular periods, painful periods, heavy bleeding and other menstrual concerns.

🌸

Fibroid Treatment

Assessment and management options for uterine fibroids based on symptoms and individual needs.

💗

Endometriosis Treatment

Evaluation and management of endometriosis, pelvic pain and related fertility concerns.

🌼

Menopause Management

Guidance for menopausal symptoms, hormonal changes and women's health during midlife.

🩺

General Gynaecology

Consultation for common gynaecological problems, reproductive health and preventive care.

🔬

Recurrent Pregnancy Loss

Assessment and counselling for couples experiencing repeated pregnancy loss.

🌷

Adolescent Gynaecology

Age-appropriate consultation for menstrual and reproductive health concerns in adolescents.

💝

Preventive Women's Healthcare

Routine gynaecological consultations, health assessments and preventive care guidance.

Advanced Gynaecological Procedures

Diagnostic Hysteroscopy

Examination of the uterine cavity for selected reproductive and gynaecological conditions.

Operative Hysteroscopy

Gynaecological Laparoscopy

Keyhole surgical options for selected pelvic and reproductive health conditions.
